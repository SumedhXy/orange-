1. EXECUTIVE ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CITRUS-TERRA AI is a ground-level, cyber-physical orchard monitoring and precision 
intervention system designed specifically for Nagpur Mandarin (Citrus reticulata) 
orchards in Vidarbha (Katol, Warud, Morshi, Amravati, Nagpur belts).

Unlike aerial drones—which physically cannot see under-leaf sucking pests or 
trunk collar gummosis and suffer from extreme capital costs and thermal limits in 
45°C heat—CITRUS-TERRA AI operates directly from the ground through a 3-component 
synergy:

   │ 1. "TRACTOR-VISION" MULTI-ANGLE CAMERA RIG                             │
   │    Mounted on existing orchard tractors/power-tillers during routine   │
   │    passes (weeding, rotavating). Inspects trunk collars and under-leaf  │
   │    canopies from lateral and upward angles.                            │
   └───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                                       │ Real-Time Inspection & Tree Indexing
   ┌───────────────────────────────────▼────────────────────────────────────┐

2. WHY GROUND-LEVEL VISION IS DECISIVELY SUPERIOR TO DRONES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Direct Line-of-Sight to Under-Leaf Sucking Pests:
   - Asian Citrus Psylla (Diaphorina citri), Citrus Blackfly/Whitefly, and Leaf 
     Miners live strictly on the UNDERSIDE of young flush leaves.
   - CITRUS-TERRA AI mounts an upper camera tilted 30° upwards with an LED ring 
     light, capturing high-resolution macro imagery of leaf undersides as the 
     vehicle moves between rows.

2. Uncompromised Trunk Collar Visibility (Phytophthora Gummosis):
   - Phytophthora attacks the trunk base (0–30 cm above ground). A lower camera 
     tilted 15° downwards inspects the bark collar directly, detecting water-soaked 
     gumming lesions before the tree suffers canopy defoliation.

3. Zero Additional Labor or Operational Fuel Costs:
   - Farmers in Vidarbha already run a tractor or power-tiller through orchard rows 
     every 15 to 20 days for harrowing (bakhar), rotavating, inter-cultivation, 
     or mowing.
   - The monitoring rig piggybacks on these routine operations. No pilot required.

4. 100% Elimination of GPS Drift Misidentifications:
   - Drones rely on raw GPS, which has ±3 to 5m drift—causing confusion between 
     trees in a 5m x 5m grid.
   - CITRUS-TERRA AI pairs a basic GPS receiver with a tractor Wheel-Encoder 
     (odometry) and an optical canopy presence sensor. Trees along a row are 
     counted deterministically: [Block A | Row 04 | Tree 12]. Zero indexing error.

5. Extreme Capital Savings (CapEx):
   - Total hardware rig cost: ₹25,000 to ₹35,000 (accessible to any individual farmer), 
     compared to ₹8,00,000–₹12,00,000 for an agricultural drone setup.


3. HARDWARE & HARDWARE INTEGRATION SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Tractor-Mounted "Tractor-Vision" Rig:
   - Mounting Frame: Adjustable, vibration-damped vertical steel mast mounted to the 
     front bumper or roll-over protective structure (ROPS) of the tractor.
   - Camera 1 (Trunk & Collar Inspector):
     * Position: 40 cm above ground level, tilted 15° downwards.
     * Lens: Wide-angle 1080p industrial global-shutter USB3 camera (60 FPS).
     * Target: Bark cracking, gum exudation, collar rot (Phytophthora).
   - Camera 2 (Canopy Profile & Under-Leaf Inspector):
     * Position: 1.6 meters above ground level, tilted 30° upwards into the canopy.
     * Lens: High-resolution camera with diffuse LED illuminator (to eliminate 
       shadows and sun glare under leaves).
     * Target: Leaf undersides (Psylla nymphs, whitefly colonies, sooty mold, canker).
   - Tree Indexing Odometry:
     * Hall-effect magnetic wheel sensor mounted on the rear tractor wheel.
     * Optical ToF (Time-of-Flight) / Ultrasonic distance sensor detecting trunk 
       passages to confirm tree transitions.

B. Cabin Edge Processing Unit:
   - Hardware: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (40 TOPS) or Raspberry Pi 5 with AI Accelerator 
     (Hailo-8 / Coral TPU).
   - Power Supply: 12V DC stepped down from the tractor battery via rugged surge protector.
   - Storage: 256GB High-endurance NVMe SSD (stores offline models, image crops, and log).
   - Connectivity: Local Wi-Fi hotspot (connects to tractor driver's Android phone).

C. Smart IoT Pest Trap Sentinels (Autonomous 24/7):
   - Structure: Weatherproof solar-powered yellow sticky / delta pheromone trap.
   - Sensor: ESP32-CAM (2MP) with ultra-low-power sleep circuit, powered by a 5W mini 
     solar panel and 18650 Li-ion battery.
   - Operation: Wakes up twice daily (morning & dusk), captures a macro photo of the 
     trap surface, runs lightweight on-chip pest counting, and transmits daily counts 
     via LoRaWAN / 4G-NB-IoT to the cabin edge unit or cloud.
   - Unit Cost: Under ₹2,200 per trap (1 trap per 3–5 acres).


4. AI COMPUTER VISION PIPELINE & MODELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All inferencing runs locally in the tractor cabin in real time (30–60 FPS):

[MODEL 1: TRUNK COLLAR GUMMOSIS DETECTOR]
- Architecture: YOLOv11n (Nano) trained on Vidarbha citrus trunk datasets.
- Input: Frame crops of the trunk base as the tractor passes each tree.
- Classes:
  1. Healthy Trunk / Clean Bark
  2. Phytophthora Gummosis (Early: water-soaked oozing gum)
  3. Advanced Gummosis (Bark splitting, collar rot girdle)
- Inference Latency: < 15ms per tree crop.

[MODEL 2: UNDER-LEAF PEST & FOLIAR PATHOLOGY DETECTOR]
- Architecture: YOLOv11s-OBB / MobileNetV4 with Attention Mechanism.
- Input: High-FPS upward-angled under-canopy image streams.
- Classes:
  1. Asian Citrus Psylla (Diaphorina citri) nymphs & waxy honeydew secretions
  2. Citrus Canker (Xanthomonas citri corky pustules with chlorotic halos)
  3. Citrus Leaf Miner (serpentine silver feeding trails)
  4. Citrus Blackfly / Sooty Mold (velvety black coating on leaves)
  5. Iron / Zinc Deficiency Chlorosis (interveinal yellowing pattern)
- Detection Accuracy: > 90% mAP@50 across field lighting conditions.

[MODEL 3: IOT SMART TRAP INSECT COUNTER]
- Architecture: Edge-optimized tiny Haar-Cascade / MobileNet-SSD.
- Counts trapped Asian Citrus Psylla and Fruit Sucking Moths.
- Compares against Economic Threshold Level (ETL):
  * Psylla ETL: > 5 adults per trap per day triggers immediate flush alert.


5. DETERMINISTIC TREE-LEVEL GIS INDEXING (NO GPS DRIFT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To completely eliminate the standard ±4m GPS drift problem:

1. Orchard Baseline Initialization:
   - When the tractor enters an orchard, the driver selects the Block (e.g., "Block A").
   - Row Entry Confirmation: The driver aligns with Row 1; GPS records entry point.

2. Distance-Based Tree Identification (Odometry + Optical Detection):
   - Orange trees in Vidarbha are planted at fixed regular intervals (e.g., 5.0m or 6.0m).
   - As the tractor rolls forward, the wheel encoder measures linear distance traveled.
   - The lateral ultrasonic/optical sensor detects the trunk passage.
   - Every detected tree is assigned an immutable ID:
     `VID_BLK_A_R03_T14` (Block A, Row 3, Tree 14).
   - Any anomaly detected by the cameras is permanently linked to this exact Tree ID.


6. TEMPORAL DELTA TRACKING & HEALTH RISK MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system maintains a local SQLite / GeoPackage database on the edge unit. 
Every 15–20 days when the tractor makes another routine pass, health metrics are 
compared against previous scans:

- Tree Health Index (0 to 100):
  * Green (85–100): Healthy foliage, no bark lesions, clean flush.
  * Yellow (65–84): Mild nutrient chlorosis or minor leaf miner damage (Monitor).
  * Orange (40–64): Active Asian Citrus Psylla colony or early canker (Action Required).
  * Red (<40): Active Phytophthora Gummosis or severe Dieback (Immediate Treatment).

- Temporal Deterioration Alert:
  * If a tree drops by > 25 points between two consecutive passes, it triggers 
    an escalated priority inspection alert.

8. PRECISION TARGETED INTERVENTION (REAL-TIME OR DIRECTED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Option 1: Real-Time Selective Tractor Spot-Spraying (Automated):
   - If the tractor is equipped with a standard rear orchard boom/mist blower 
     (e.g., Mitra / ASPEE orchard sprayer), the edge compute unit is wired to 
     a 12V relay box controlling individual solenoid valves.
   - When the camera detects Psylla or Canker on a tree, it opens the spray nozzle 
     ONLY for the 1.5 seconds it takes to pass that specific canopy.
   - Result: 65% to 75% reduction in pesticide spray volume.

B. Option 2: Geo-Targeted Manual Treatment for Gummosis:
   - Bark rot requires manual application of thick Bordeaux paste (it cannot be 
     sprayed through a mist nozzle).
   - The system generates a sorted "Task List" of exact Tree IDs requiring Bordeaux 
     painting, allowing a single laborer to treat 50 infected trees in 1 hour instead 
     of spending 2 days wandering across 3,000 trees.
