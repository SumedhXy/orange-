PROBLEM STATEMENT: AI-ENABLED PRECISION MONITORING & TARGETED INTERVENTION 
SYSTEM FOR LARGE ORANGE ORCHARDS IN VIDARBHA (GROUND-LEVEL VISION & IOT)
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE CONTEXT & BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Vidarbha region of Maharashtra (Nagpur, Amravati, Wardha, Katol, Kalmeshwar, 
Morshi, and Warud belts) is the premier hub for Nagpur Mandarin (Citrus reticulata 
Blanco) cultivation. Orchards typically span 10 to 50+ acres with tree spacing 
arranged in square grids (typically 5m x 5m or 6m x 6m).

Despite strong market demand, citrus growers in Vidarbha face an agronomic 
crisis termed "Citrus Decline". Orchards suffer from devastating pest infestations, 
fungal bark infections, micronutrient deficiencies, and severe heat/moisture stress.

Conventional management relies on manual visual inspections by farm laborers walking 
rows of thousands of trees. This process is slow, expensive, subjective, and 
fails to detect early-stage infections before they become widespread. Consequently, 
growers resort to blanket chemical spraying, driving up input costs, inducing pest 
resistance, and polluting groundwater.


2. THE CRITICAL BOTANICAL & MONITORING BOTTLENECK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aerial monitoring tools (such as top-down drones) face fundamental physical and 
pathological limitations in citrus orchards:
1. Under-Leaf Habitat of Sucking Pests:
   - Key pests—such as Asian Citrus Psylla (Diaphorina citri, the vector for greening), 
     Citrus Blackfly/Whitefly, and Leaf Miners—lay eggs and feed strictly on the 
     UNDERSIDE of young leaves and inside inner canopies. Top-down aerial views 
     cannot detect these pests until secondary symptoms (defoliation) appear.
2. Trunk-Level Pathologies:
   - Phytophthora Gummosis (Foot Rot)—the leading tree killer in Vidarbha—originates 
     at the trunk collar (0–30 cm above ground level). Dense spherical orange canopies 
     completely obscure the trunk from above.
3. Drone Economics & Heat Constraints:
   - High capital costs (₹6L–15L+), licensed pilot requirements, battery degradation 
     in 45°C Vidarbha summers, and lack of spray penetration make drones unviable for 
     routine small-to-medium grower adoption.

Therefore, the system MUST prioritize Ground-Level Multi-Angle Optical Sensing, 
Automated IoT Trapping, and Mobile Precision Diagnostics.


3. CORE OBJECTIVES & PROBLEM SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Develop a low-cost, ground-enabled, AI-driven cyber-physical system for large 
Vidarbha orange orchards that can:
1. Monitor individual tree foliage (including leaf undersides) and trunk collars 
   without aerial drone dependencies.
2. Detect potential pests (Psylla, Whitefly, Leaf Miner), diseases (Gummosis, Canker, 
   Greening/HLB), and physiological stress at an early stage.
3. Precisely locate and index every tree to eliminate GPS drift misidentifications.
4. Track historical health deterioration through periodic scans across flowering cycles.
5. Generate actionable, localized alerts and treatment recommendations in Marathi and Hindi.
6. Support targeted, precision interventions (spot spraying / trunk paste applications) 
   to slash chemical use and manual labor.


4. SPECIFIC PATHOLOGICAL & STRESS CONDITIONS TO TARGET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Based on guidelines from ICAR - Central Citrus Research Institute (ICAR-CCRI, Nagpur):

A. Sucking Pests & Vectors (Under-Leaf & Foliage):
   - Asian Citrus Psylla (Diaphorina citri): Nymphs on tender leaf undersides; HLB vector.
   - Citrus Blackfly / Whitefly (Aleurocanthus woglumi): Nymph colonies and sooty mold.
   - Citrus Leaf Miner (Phyllocnistis citrella): Serpentine mines on lower leaf surfaces.
   - Fruit Sucking Moth: Punctures fruit during evening/night hours.

B. Trunk Collar & Soil-Borne Pathologies:
   - Phytophthora Gummosis & Foot Rot: Water-soaked bark lesions, oozing brown gum, 
     and bark splitting at the lower trunk collar.

C. Foliar, Branch & Systemic Pathologies:
   - Citrus Canker (Xanthomonas citri): Raised corky lesions with water-soaked halos.
   - Citrus Greening (HLB): Asymmetric blotchy mottle, vein corking, chlorosis.
   - Dieback (Twig Blight): Drying of twigs from outer branches inwards.

D. Nutritional & Physiological Stresses:
   - Iron (Fe) & Zinc (Zn) Chlorosis: Interveinal yellowing on alkaline black cotton soils.
   - Severe Heat & Moisture Stress: Leaf curling and wilting in 45°C+ summer conditions.


5. DETAILED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ground-Level Multi-Angle Optical Scanning:
   - Capture high-resolution imagery of:
     a) Leaf undersides and inner canopy foliage.
     b) Trunk collars and ground graft unions.
   - Capable of being mounted on existing orchard machinery (tractors, power-tillers, 
     or small ground rovers) used during routine orchard operations (rotavating, weeding).

2. Automated Pest Population Surveillance (Smart IoT Traps):
   - Fixed, low-cost solar-powered optical traps (yellow sticky/pheromone) deployed 
     across orchard blocks to detect insect surges (Psylla, Fruit Flies) before 
     visible canopy symptoms emerge.

3. Tree-Level Geospatial Location Mapping:
   - Overcome consumer GPS drift (±3 to 5m) using combined GPS + wheel odometry or 
     optical tree-counter indexing.
   - Ensure 100% accurate tree identification (e.g., Block B, Row 04, Tree 18).

4. Periodic Health Comparison & Risk Stratification:
   - Compare scans across 15-day/30-day intervals to identify dynamic deterioration.
   - Classify trees into:
     * Healthy (Green)
     * Mild Stress / Monitor (Yellow)
     * High Risk / Pest Infested (Orange)
     * Critical / Gummosis or Severe Dieback (Red)

5. Actionable Vernacular Alerts (Marathi & Hindi):
   - Deliver push notifications/WhatsApp advisories detailing:
     * Exact Tree/Row Location.
     * Detected Pest/Disease & Confidence Score.
     * ICAR-CCRI approved chemical, bio-agent, or cultural remedy.

6. Precision Targeted Intervention:
   - Guide tractor-mounted spot-sprayers (solenoid nozzle control) or manual crews 
     directly to tagged trees, reducing pesticide consumption by >=65%.

7. Offline & Rural-Resilient Architecture:
   - Zero dependence on cloud upload or rural 4G during scanning.
   - Local edge inference (in-cabin or on-device) completes analysis in real time.

8. Low-Cost Scalability:
   - Hardware rig cost under ₹35,000 (accessible to individual growers or FPOs).
   - Zero additional labor required if integrated into existing tractor passes.
